## The framing difference to hold onto

Digital-minds motivation: welfare / moral status ("does the model *experience*
distress?"). Ours: safety / faithfulness ("can the verbal channel be trusted
as a monitor?"). **Same instruments, different why.** For Neel, keep the
safety/faithfulness framing — his vocabulary is model character, self-models,
introspection reliability (Binder et al.), not welfare. Do not let the
write-up drift into consciousness/welfare language; it pattern-matches away
from his interests and toward claims the data can't support.
